Attitudes towards Hyperpersonalisation

Definition: Hyperpersonalization uses generative AI to tailor news to individuals’ preferences and present it in flexible formats and ways. For example, generative AI can turn a news article into an audio clip or a short summary, making it more engaging and relevant for different individuals.
