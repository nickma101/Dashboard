Trust in News Media

To measure three levels of trust in news media, we asked respondents: “On a scale from 0 to 10, where 0 means”no trust at all” and 10 means “complete trust”, how much do you trust the news media in your country?“

For illustration, we combined scores into low (0-3), medium (4-7) and high (8-10) levels of trust.
