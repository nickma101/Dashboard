AI Journalism Attitudes

To measure Willingness to Engage, we asked respondents: “If you know from the beginning that journalistic content is generated with the help of AI, how willing are you to …”

To measure Attitudes towards Hyperpersonalisation, we asked respondents: “Hyper-personalization uses generative AI to tailor news to your preferences and present it in flexible formatsways. For example, generative AI can change turn a news article into an audio clip or a summary, making it more engaging and relevant for you. To what extent do you agree with the following statements?”

To measure Aspects influencing Credibility Judgements, we asked respondents: “When you read an article that may be written by generative AI, how does each of the following factors influence how much you believe the content?”
